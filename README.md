# Multi-Agent Equity Research System

An AI-powered equity research platform that uses multiple specialized LLM agents to analyze company fundamentals, technical indicators, recent news, and investment risks before generating a structured investment research recommendation.

## Project Overview

This project is inspired by multi-agent financial research systems such as TradingAgents.

Instead of relying on a single AI model to make an investment judgment, the system assigns different analytical responsibilities to specialized agents.

The current version includes:

- Fundamental Analyst
- Technical Analyst
- News & Sentiment Analyst
- Decision Agent

Each analyst independently evaluates a different dimension of the stock before the Decision Agent synthesizes the findings into a final research recommendation.

## Agents

### Fundamental Analyst

Analyzes:

- P/E ratio
- Revenue growth
- Profit margin
- Market capitalization
- 52-week price range
- Fundamental strengths
- Fundamental risks

Produces:

- Fundamental Score
- Bullish / Neutral / Bearish view
- Fundamental research report

### Technical Analyst

Analyzes:

- Current price
- MA20
- MA50
- RSI
- MACD
- MACD signal
- Trading volume
- 52-week price range

Produces:

- Technical Score
- Bullish / Neutral / Bearish view
- Technical research report

### News Analyst

Analyzes recent market headlines and evaluates:

- Overall news sentiment
- Positive catalysts
- Negative catalysts
- Company developments
- Short-term investor implications
- News-related risks

Produces:

- News Score
- Positive / Neutral / Negative sentiment
- News research report

### Decision Agent

Reviews the reports generated by all specialist agents.

Produces:

- Bull Case
- Bear Case
- Key Risks
- Final Investment Thesis
- BUY / HOLD / SELL rating
- Confidence Score
- Risk Level

## Data Pipeline

Market data is collected using Python and Yahoo Finance through the `yfinance` library.

Technical indicators are calculated directly in Python.

Current indicators include:

- MA20
- MA50
- RSI
- MACD
- MACD Signal

The structured market data is then passed to the relevant AI agents.
